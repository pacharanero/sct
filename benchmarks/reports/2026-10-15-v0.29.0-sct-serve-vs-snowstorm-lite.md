# `sct serve` 0.29.0 versus Snowstorm Lite 2.5.0

**Environment:** UK Monolith 42.3.0 (2026-07-01), 837,930 active concepts; Linux 7.2.9-2-cachyos; Intel Core Ultra 9 185H; 64 GB RAM; NVMe SSD; Hyperfine 2.0.0; 20 measured runs after 5 warmups.

Both servers ran on the same host and served the same release over separate loopback FHIR endpoints. Snowstorm Lite used image `snomedinternational/snowstorm-lite@sha256:892fcdc8f482c5ec0480d67cba4271669ea06b54ec73738bba02322ad2c89c11` with `-Xms8g -Xmx16g`.
